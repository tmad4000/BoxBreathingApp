# Box Breathing

Intent: someone taking a short breathing break needs clear pacing, quiet progress and a natural stopping point.
Hierarchy: phase and countdown lead; completed rounds and four progress markers support; one full-width primary action.
Palette: preserve the established indigo/violet identity, consolidated to #141426 canvas, #202037 surface, #BBB5FF accent and #B9B8CC secondary text. Accent follows the breath and marks completed rounds.
Depth: tonal surfaces and fine geometry, no decorative pulse or heavy shadows.
Surfaces: a dark neutral canvas with a slightly lighter information sheet.
Typography: native sans-serif; 26/600 heading, 20 phase, 68/300 countdown, 16 progress, 15 secondary; tabular dynamic numerals.
Spacing: 4-point base, 24 horizontal inset and section gaps, 52-point primary action. Scrollable at small sizes and large text. Controls use native Flutter button semantics.

Signature: four sides, four seconds, four visible progress markers, four rounds per checkpoint. Stop after the final hold; continue in groups of four while retaining the session total. No target settings required.
Motion: a single phase clock drives countdown and edge tracer; pause preserves fractional progress. Reduce Motion removes tracer movement. No idle pulse.
